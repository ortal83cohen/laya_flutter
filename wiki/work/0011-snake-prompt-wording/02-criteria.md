## Explicitly not required

- Higher model confidence or longer survival after the wording change.
- Measuring multilingual tokenizer token counts under the option encode cap.
- On-disk rl_agent_config head_max_len or max_len overrides.
- Explaining why a device log showed head (2,0).
- Moving the omitted-initial-snake opening away from the board center.
- Changing board size, restart-after-death, or adding a step timer.
- Changing library tokenizer, open, or predict.
- Adding a post-predict safety shield or absolute, opaque, or boolean keys.
- Reopening or changing the macOS ONNX session sandbox solution.
