## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-28

### Added

- Initial pub.dev release.
- `LayaFlutter.open` with multilingual ONNX checkpoint download and cache.
- `LayaFlutter.openLocalBundle` for task-specific ONNX bundles.
- `LoadedRuntime.predict` for choice, score, and noul questions.
- Example app: classic Snake driven by offline Laya predict.
- README, screenshot GIF, and pub.dev metadata.
